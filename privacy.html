What information is collected?

For my root domain (floofie.org), I run my website through Cloudflare Pages. I'm sure there's some sort of analytics through Cloudflare, but I can't be bothered to look for it. For what data Cloudflare may collect, take a look at their privacy policy.

For any of my subdomains (*.floofie.org), I do log any requests made. An example log of a request looks like this:

{"level":"info","ts":1234567890,"logger":"http.log.access.log0","msg":"handled request","request":{"remote_ip":"127.0.0.1","remote_port":"12345","client_ip":"1","proto":"HTTP/1.1","method":"GET","host":"example.com","uri":"/","headers":{"User-Agent":["curl/8.14.1"],"Accept":["*/*"]}},"bytes_read":0,"user_id":"","duration":0.000074682,"size":0,"status":200,"resp_headers":{"Content-Type":[],"Server":["Caddy"],"Connection":["close"],"Location":["https://example.com/"]}}

The information included is:

  • Time of request
  • IP addresses
  • User Agent
  • Request method, URI, data, and whatever my server response back with

This is stored for ~30 days at most, unless I delete it earlier.

What is this information used for?

If you're a normal user, mostly nothing. I seldomly look at any of the logs except through goaccess.

If any attempted abuse on my services is detected, you could be blocked and/or reported to AbuseIPDB.

How do I know you won't sell my information?

You can't, but I have zero reason to sell any information.


for any concerns, you can contact me.
